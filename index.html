My research spans time-domain astrophysics, supernovae spectroscopy, and machine learning architectures for astronomical alert streams. I build Graph Neural Network (GNN) pipelines for transient classification (Fink Science Module for ZTF/LSST), perform radiative-transfer modeling with CMFGEN, and run hydrodynamical/MESA simulations to study stellar explosions, exoplanet atmospheres, and cosmological signals.
